The model's power rating for every FBS team: points better than an average FBS team on a neutral field.

Reading the ratings

Power blends four ratings, weighted by how well each predicted past margins. Subtract two teams' power ratings and add about 3 points for the home team to get the model's line.

Elo moves after every game based on the result and the margin (1500 is average). Scoring is opponent-adjusted points margin. EPA is opponent-adjusted expected points per play, offense minus defense, scaled to about 70 plays. Success is opponent-adjusted success rate (offense minus defense, in percentage points). Each starts the season from a prior built on last season, returning production, recruiting and the transfer portal.

QB shows Change when the team's passer last game threw under half of its passes this season, so its ratings were mostly built with someone else at quarterback. The model takes about half a point off for it.

3 wk is the change in power over the last three weeks. SP+ and FPI are well-known public ratings, shown for comparison only; the model doesn't use them.
