Reading the board

Model is the model's line for the favorite (TEX −16.2 means Texas by 16.2). Win is the favorite's chance to win.

Market is the middle line across US sportsbooks at the latest snapshot, with where it opened underneath. Edge is the part of the gap between the model and the market that the model trusts, with the side it likes and the share of the gap it trusts in that kind of game. Past seasons showed most of the gap is the model's own error, so it trusts about 17% in Power 4 games and less in Group of 5 games, after a quarterback change, and early in the season.

QB change marks a team whose passer last game threw under half of its passes this season, so its ratings were mostly built with someone else at quarterback.

Games with a pick have an amber border. After a game, the final score and which side covered the market's last line are shown.

Data via CollegeFootballData.com and The Odds API. For entertainment; not betting advice.
