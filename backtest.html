Backtest

Before any pick counts, the model had to beat past seasons' betting lines under rules written down before it ran.

The go-live bar

    By season

    Model v3. Each season was tested with a model fit only on the seasons before it.

    Every version

    The same test for v2 and for each v3 change on its own, for comparison. Only v3 as a whole was the decision.

    Accuracy

    Average miss on the final margin, in points (lower is better). The market's lines are the bar to beat.

    How it was tested

    Walk-forward. Every prediction uses only games played before it, and each test season's model, trust factors, edge threshold and start week were chosen from earlier seasons only. Ratings are fit from 2016 on; opening lines exist only from 2021, so 2022 is the first season that could be tested.

    Bet at the opener. A backtest pick is placed at the opening line (median of US books) and graded against it at −110. Against the close grades the same picks at the closing line: around 50% there means the model knows nothing the final market price doesn't, and any profit comes from getting ahead of the line move.

    The cautious check reruns the picks ignoring the most recent week's games, in case some opening lines were posted before those games were played. Its line move had to stay positive too.

    Three models were tried. v1 used Elo and a scoring-margin rating. v2 added play-by-play efficiency and preseason priors from returning production, recruiting and the transfer portal; it missed the bar. v3 adds two fixes aimed at why v2's picks lost: a quarterback-change flag, and trust, which keeps only the share of the model's gap from the market that held up in past seasons, less in Group of 5 games, after a QB change and early in the season. v3 passed, but because it's the third try on the same seasons its picks stay provisional until the live record backs it up.
